About Us

The Future of Video Creation

VisionAI is on a mission to democratize video creation through the power of artificial intelligence. We believe everyone deserves to tell their story through stunning, professional video.

Our Story

VisionAI was founded in 2022 by a team of AI researchers, video producers, and technology entrepreneurs who shared a common vision: to make professional video creation accessible to everyone.

We saw that creating high-quality videos required expensive equipment, specialized skills, and countless hours of editing. We set out to change that by building the most advanced AI video generation platform in the world.

Today, VisionAI powers over 2.5 million video creations for creators, businesses, and organizations across 150+ countries. Our AI models continue to evolve, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in AI-powered video.
