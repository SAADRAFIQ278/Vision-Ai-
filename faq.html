Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about VisionAI. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact our support team.

General

VisionAI is a cutting-edge AI video generation platform that turns text prompts into professional-quality videos using advanced artificial intelligence models. It's designed for creators, marketers, and businesses of all sizes.

Simply describe your vision in a text prompt, and VisionAI's AI models analyze your description to generate a fully-produced video with matching visuals, motion, and style. Our advanced models understand context, lighting, composition, and cinematic techniques.

VisionAI is designed for content creators, marketers, video producers, businesses, educators, and anyone who wants to create professional videos without the need for expensive equipment or specialized editing skills.

Technical

VisionAI supports export up to 4K resolution with cinematic quality on the Pro plan, and 8K on the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan supports up to 720p. Quality also depends on the AI model you choose.

Generation time depends on the model and resolution. VisionAI-Lite can generate a 30-second video in ~6 seconds, VisionAI-Studio in ~12.5 seconds, and VisionAI-Pro in ~22.5 seconds.

VisionAI supports MP4, MOV, WebM, GIF, and individual frame sequences in PNG or JPEG formats. You can choose the format that best suits your needs.
